# Prepare a fan chart for 7.3.1

[Tiếng Việt](vi/09-add-fan-chart.md)

Prepare the AFF chart, OGG audio, jacket and song metadata. Choose unique song and pack IDs in the 7.3.1 catalogue. Adding a chart requires an authenticated bundle with matching songlist, packlist and unlocks, plus corresponding database entitlements. Once the bundle is ready, use [the release chapter](07-build-release-content-bundle.md) to stage it and check the download.

The public workflow does not yet generate a 7.3.1 bundle from loose files.
